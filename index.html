Cattle sourced the way you would source them yourself

Thigpen Livestock Company was founded by Robby Thigpen and is now in its third generation of family ownership. We buy cattle on order from the finest ranches and auction houses across Texas and the surrounding states, matching every load to the weight, quality and delivery window our customers ask for.
